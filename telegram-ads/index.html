Questions

Is NomardDesk part of Telegram?

No. NomardDesk is an independent agency. We run your ads through the official Telegram Ad Platform.

Can you fix my rejected Telegram ad?

Most rejections happen because the ad, the image, or the destination don't match, or the channel looks unfinished. We find the cause, fix it, and resubmit. If the offer itself breaks Telegram's rules, such as promising guaranteed income, we tell you honestly instead of taking your money.

What do I need before I start?

A channel, bot, or group with a profile photo, a full description, and real posts. You also need an ad budget, which is paid in TON on the Telegram Ad Platform.

How much ad budget do I need?

It depends on your country, your topic, and the channels you target. We start with a small test, keep what works, and move more budget into the channels that bring results.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Our fee covers the work. Your ad budget is separate and is paid to Telegram.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee sales. What we promise is a clean setup, careful testing, clear reports, and straight answers about what is and isn't working.
